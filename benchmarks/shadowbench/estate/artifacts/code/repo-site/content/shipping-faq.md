# Shipping FAQ

## When is the booking cut-off?

15:00 local time at the origin terminal, one business day before
departure. Reefer bookings close at 12:00.

## How do I label pallets?

One SSCC label per pallet face, both long sides. Labels are validated at
the receiving gate; unreadable labels go to the exceptions bay.

## Who do I call about a delayed trailer?

The dispatch desk at your origin terminal owns the trailer until handover;
their number is on the booking confirmation.
